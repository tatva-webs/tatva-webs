01 / GENERAL
What is Tatva Webs?

Tatva Webs is a digital web design and development studio focused on creating modern, responsive and purposeful websites for businesses and individuals.

What kind of websites do you create?

We work on business websites, portfolio websites, landing pages, creative websites and other custom web experiences based on project requirements.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

Not at all. We'll guide you through the process, explain important decisions in simple language and keep you updated as the project progresses.

02 / PRICING & PAYMENT
How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, design complexity, features and integrations. Our pricing page shows indicative starting packages. We'll share a clear quotation after understanding your requirements.

Are there any hidden charges?

We aim to keep pricing transparent. Any additional services, third-party tools, domain or hosting costs will be discussed before they are included.

Do you offer custom packages?

Yes. If your project doesn't fit one of our standard packages, we can discuss a custom solution based on your goals and budget.

03 / PROJECT & DELIVERY
How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project. A smaller website may take less time than a larger custom experience. We'll agree on an estimated timeline before starting.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Responsive design is an important part of our approach. We build layouts that adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Can I request changes during development?

Yes. Feedback and revisions are part of the process. The number of included revision rounds depends on the package or project agreement. Larger changes may require additional time or cost.

Will I own my website after completion?

Once the agreed payment and handover are complete, the finished project files and agreed deliverables will be handed over to you. Third-party services remain subject to their own terms.

04 / SUPPORT
Do you provide website maintenance?

Maintenance and ongoing support can be discussed separately. We can help with updates, improvements and other agreed maintenance requirements.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We can review your existing website, understand what needs improvement and discuss a redesign that better supports your goals.
